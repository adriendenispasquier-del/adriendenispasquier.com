Adrien Pasquier

Rocket Wine

Event posters - Illustration

I have been collaborating with Jeff, co-founder of Rocket Wine, for several years. This ongoing exchange led to the creation of multiple projects together, notably for the Wine Rush festival.

For this series, I explored a new graphic direction inspired by painting techniques and layered color application. The visual language was built through successive layers, allowing depth and vibration to emerge rather than relying on flat construction.
