Adrien Pasquier

Archipel Still Life

Product photography - Set design

Products were treated as sculptural forms. Light was constructed carefully, warm, directional, suggestive, allowing viewers to sense an environment beyond the frame: a Mediterranean terrace, a quiet Sunday kitchen, a summer afternoon filtered through linen curtains.
